# About Rich Haase

This repository is a public AboutMe and working-with-me guide. It is meant for anyone who wants useful context about how I think about leadership, collaboration, and support.

## What This Is

This is not a full biography, resume, or work history. It is a small public guide to how I try to work with people.

The short version:

* hire great people and get out of their way
* make expectations and constraints clear
* support people as they learn, grow, and pursue goals that matter to them
* protect people's time and attention where possible
* listen more and talk less

## How To Work With Me

I value open communication. Tell me what is working and what is not.

My style is more hands-off by default, so it is useful to calibrate directly. If you need more time, context, support, or feedback from me, say so.

I care about people having room to do good work without sacrificing their lives outside work.

## What I Try To Provide

I try to:

* set a clear vision and help the team determine the path toward it
* frame business problems and constraints so success is easier to understand
* support learning, growth, and meaningful goal-setting
* educate the broader organization about what my team can provide
* encourage communication and collaboration across the organization
* create an environment where strong people can thrive

## What I Ask From Others

I ask the people I support to:

* communicate openly
* say when something is not working
* take care of themselves and their teammates
* treat work as important, but not more important than family, friends, health, or a life outside the job
